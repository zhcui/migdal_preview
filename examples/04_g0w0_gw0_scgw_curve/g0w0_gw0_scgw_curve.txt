Example 04: G0W0/GW0/scGW density-curve tutorial

Question:
  How does the leading pairing eigenvalue lambda(n) change when the
  normal-state Green's function and screened interaction are updated
  self-consistently?

Density scan:
  6 points from 1.000e+23 down to 1.000e+16 cm^-3

Methods:
  G0W0: fixed G0 and fixed RPA W0.
  GW0: self-consistent normal-state G with fixed W0.
  scGW: self-consistent normal-state G and W.

Driver settings used:
  All three drivers use no-truncation gap solves and
  diag_average='auto', which selects diagonal cell averaging
  for this no-truncation comparison.
  q_interp_mode=q_singular, diag_average_mode=auto
  sparse-IR file=examples/04_g0w0_gw0_scgw_curve/ir.5

SCF settings:
  GW0 inserted-continuation max_dex=1.0, restart=off, max_cycle=300.
  scGW inserted-continuation max_dex=1.0, restart=off.
  scGW damp=0.98, DIIS space=8.
  scGW DIIS start=0, max_cycle=300.
  Fermi-surface pin: on; unconverged rows are recorded with flags.

Result snapshot:
  G0W0 lambda range: 0.725392 to 4.68246
  GW0 lambda range: 0.5285 to 1.51519
  scGW lambda range: 0.498818 to 1.33281

Convergence summary:
  G0W0: gap 6/6, no normal-state SCF loop
  GW0: gap 6/6, SCF 6/6, cycles 8-14
  scGW: gap 6/6, SCF 6/6, cycles 11-17
