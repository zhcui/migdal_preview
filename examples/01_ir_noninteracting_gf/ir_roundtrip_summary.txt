Example 01: sparse-IR round trip for G0

Model:
  G0(k, iw) = 1 / (iw - (e_k - mu))
  e_k [Ha] = [-0.3  -0.08  0.    0.07  0.25]
  mu  [Ha] = 0.015

Sparse-IR grid:
  beta = 2500 Ha^-1, lambda = 1000, tol = 1.0e-12
  60 fermion frequencies, 60 fermion tau points

Round trip:
  G0(iw) -> G0(tau) -> G0(iw)
  max absolute error = 2.913442e-13
  max relative error = 4.385472e-15
