Example 09: multi-phonon-mode density-curve tutorial

Question:
  How do the G0W0, GW0, and scGW density curves change when the
  lattice factor uses three explicit phonon poles instead of the
  default compressed single pole?

Phonon model:
  variant=3_mode_dielectric_factor_TO_anchored
  source=paper_git/calc/3_mode/_params.py
  F3(i nu)=prod_j (nu^2 + TO_j^2) / prod_j (nu^2 + LO_j^2)
  m*=2.1, eps_inf=6.16308509, eps0=23000
  TO meV: 1.238574718, 22.38840563, 73.24152018
  LO meV: 21.80125087, 55.71866263, 102.1375574
  generalized LST: prod((LO/TO)^2)=3731.89720271, eps0/eps_inf=3731.89720271

Density scan:
  6 points from 1.000e+23 down to 1.000e+16 cm^-3

Methods:
  G0W0: fixed G0 and fixed RPA W0.
  GW0: self-consistent normal-state G with fixed W0.
  scGW: self-consistent normal-state G and W.

Driver settings used:
  All three drivers use no-truncation gap solves and
  diag_average='auto', which selects diagonal cell averaging
  for this no-truncation comparison.
  q_interp_mode=q_singular, diag_average_mode=auto
  sparse-IR file=examples/04_g0w0_gw0_scgw_curve/ir.5

SCF settings:
  GW0 restart=off, max_cycle=300, damp=0.98, DIIS space=8.
  GW0 inserted-continuation max_dex=1.0.
  scGW restart=off, max_cycle=300, damp=0.98, DIIS space=8.
  scGW inserted-continuation max_dex=1.0.
  Fermi-surface pin: on; unconverged rows are recorded with flags.

Result snapshot:
  G0W0 lambda range: 0.687544 to 3.43662
  GW0 lambda range: 0.513509 to 1.37046
  scGW lambda range: 0.484062 to 1.2075

Convergence summary:
  G0W0: gap 6/6, no normal-state SCF loop
  GW0: gap 6/6, SCF 6/6, cycles 7-12
  scGW: gap 6/6, SCF 6/6, cycles 9-14
